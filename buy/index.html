Which Format Is Right for You?

A quick guide to help you decide.

Choose Kindle if...

  • You want to start reading immediately.
  • You travel often and prefer carrying a digital library.
  • You are a Kindle Unlimited subscriber (read for free).
  • You like adjusting the font size and using the dictionary feature.

Choose Audiobook if...

  • You have a long commute or spend a lot of time driving.
  • You prefer to listen while walking, exercising, or doing chores.
  • You retain information better when you hear it spoken.
  • You enjoy a professionally narrated performance (3 hours 19 minutes).

Choose Paperback if...

  • You like to physically highlight, underline, and take notes in the margins.
  • You prefer the feel of a real book in your hands.
  • You want to easily share the book with a family member or friend.
  • You want to leave it on your coffee table as a reference guide.

Choose Hardcover if...

  • You want a durable edition that will last for years.
  • You are buying the book as a gift for someone special.
  • You are building a permanent personal library.
