The state of healthcare in the United States and what you will get out of the book. This chapter sets the stage for the rest of the playbook.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • The current landscape of the U.S. healthcare system.
  • How the system is structured and why it can feel overwhelming.
  • What practical knowledge you will gain to navigate it.

Best for: Anyone who feels confused by the system and wants a foundational overview.

Who the providers are and how they communicate with each other. From primary care doctors to specialists, nurses, therapists, care managers, and pharmacists.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • The different roles of medical professionals.
  • How your care team communicates behind the scenes.
  • Who to contact for specific medical questions.

Best for: Patients who want to understand who is actually treating them.

Payment systems, insurance coverage, self-pay, and how to understand and use your plan. This chapter covers the options for coverage, what affects the cost of care, and how to reduce costs.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • How to decode insurance terminology.
  • What affects the cost of care and how to reduce it.
  • What to do if you have trouble paying for treatment.

Best for: Caregivers and patients dealing with billing trouble or choosing a new insurance plan.

A deeper look at specialists, doctors who treat different parts of the body, cancer specialists, and surgeons. How to find the right specialist and how to get past insurance hurdles.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • How to identify the right specialist for your condition.
  • How to get a referral past insurance hurdles.
  • What to expect during a specialist consultation.

Best for: Anyone facing a new diagnosis that requires specialized care.

What can happen in a US hospital, the different staff and what they do, how the emergency room, outpatient surgery, and the main hospital work. How insurance is applied to the cost of care, and what to do without insurance.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • How the emergency room and hospital admissions process works.
  • Who the different hospital staff members are.
  • How insurance applies to your hospital bill.

Best for: Patients preparing for a hospital stay or emergency room visit.

The discharge process, what to prepare before and after, how the hospital stay affects medications, what to do if you do not feel ready to leave, and the services and post-care facilities you may need.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • How to prepare your home for a safe recovery.
  • What to review on your discharge paperwork.
  • What to do if you feel you are being discharged too early.

Best for: Caregivers and patients transitioning back home.

Options for living at home, with relatives, and the choices available when you cannot take care of yourself. This chapter helps you navigate the difficult decisions around long-term care.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • The options for long-term care facilities.
  • How to evaluate a skilled nursing unit.
  • How to plan for a parent who can no longer live independently.

Best for: Adult children of aging parents facing long-term care decisions.

How to stand up for yourself, take part in decisions about your care, and the barriers that can get in the way. This is the core of becoming your own best advocate.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • Your rights as a patient in the US.
  • How to effectively communicate with your care team.
  • How to ask questions without feeling like a burden.

Best for: Anyone who has ever felt dismissed or overwhelmed by a doctor's appointment.

Gerald's reflections across his career, from his first year as a pharmaceutical representative, before Google, to more than 27 years later, and how technology and regulation have changed the way Americans are treated.

In this chapter you will learn:

  • How the healthcare landscape has shifted over the last 27 years.
  • What technology means for the future of patient care.
  • How to apply these lessons to your own long-term health.

Best for: Readers looking for the big-picture takeaway and long-term strategies.
