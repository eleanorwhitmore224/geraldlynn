About the Book

Surviving the U.S. Healthcare System: the Patient's Playbook pulls back the curtain on what really happens in hospitals, doctors' offices, skilled nursing units and long-term care facilities. It covers misdiagnoses, medication errors, drug interactions and insurance denials, and shows how to recognize and avoid them.
Gerald Lynn, C.M.R. is a Certified Medical Representative who spent 27 years in the pharmaceutical industry. He was also the sole caregiver for his mother until she passed at age 92.
Yes. Gerald writes from both his professional experience working inside the healthcare industry and his deeply personal experience as a caregiver.

Using the Book

There are 9 chapters. They cover topics ranging from understanding the system and navigating insurance to entering the hospital, patient advocacy, and preparing for the future.
The book is designed to be read in order, but it is also a reference guide. If you are facing a specific situation right now, you can jump directly to the chapter that matches your need using the guide on the Chapters page.
No. The book is for every stage of life: in your 20s, in the middle, in the moment. It is for anyone who wants to be prepared before they need the system.
Yes. Preparing ahead of time is one of the best ways to protect yourself. The book helps you organize your medical information, understand your coverage, and prepare before a crisis strikes.
