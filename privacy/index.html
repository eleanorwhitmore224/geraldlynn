Privacy Policy

This page explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated: January 1, 2025

The Short Version

Plain-Language Summary

This website is here to share information about Gerald Lynn, his book, and his work. The site is designed to be simple and respectful of your privacy.

  • We only collect information you choose to share with us, such as your name and email address when you fill out a form.
  • We use that information to respond to your message, send updates you asked for, or improve the site.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time.
  • This site uses basic, privacy-respecting tools to function. Details are below.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you use a form on this site, you may choose to share personal information. This includes:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Organization name (when provided)
  • Event details and message content you submit
  • Any other details you choose to include in a form field

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, this site may receive basic technical information when you visit. This can include:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and general category
  • Pages viewed and approximate time on page
  • Referring website or search term, if applicable
  • A truncated or anonymized IP address, depending on the hosting setup

Information You Do Not Need to Provide

You can browse this website without submitting a form or sharing personal information. There is no account or login required.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through this website is used only for the purposes described below.

To Respond to Your Message

If you submit the contact form or speaking request form, your information is used to reply to your inquiry, answer questions, and coordinate event details.

To Send Updates You Requested

If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is used to send book updates, healthcare tips, and event announcements. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

To Improve the Website

Basic technical information may be reviewed in aggregate to understand which pages are useful, fix broken links, and improve accessibility.

To Protect the Site

Basic information may be used to detect spam, abuse, or malicious activity against the site or its users.

3. Cookies and Tracking

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your browser. Cookies help websites remember basic details between visits.

What This Site Uses

This site is designed to work without advertising cookies. It may use:

  • Essential cookies: Used only if needed for basic site functionality.
  • Privacy-respecting analytics: If analytics are enabled, they are configured to avoid identifying individual visitors where possible.

What This Site Does Not Do

  • It does not sell your data.
  • It does not use cross-site advertising trackers to build a profile of you.
  • It does not require you to accept tracking cookies to read content.

Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may change how some parts of the site work. Your browser's help section will walk you through the steps.

4. Third-Party Services

This site uses a small number of trusted third parties to operate. Each has its own privacy policy, and each is listed here for transparency.

Website Hosting and Delivery

The site is hosted and delivered through a third-party hosting provider. Standard server logs may be generated for security and reliability.

Google Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font, it may share your IP address and browser details with Google. You can learn more in Google's privacy policy.

Email Delivery and Newsletter

Email messages and newsletters are delivered through a third-party email service. That provider processes the email addresses and message content needed to send and track delivery.

External Book Retailers

Links to Amazon, Audible, Barnes and Noble, Goodreads, and similar retailers lead to external websites. Your activity on those sites is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Email Newsletter

The newsletter is optional. It is used to share book news, healthcare tips, and event announcements.

  • You can subscribe by submitting your email address in the footer form.
  • You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
  • Your email address is not sold or shared with advertisers.
  • You can also ask to be removed by contacting us directly.

If you unsubscribe, your email address may be retained on a suppression list so that you are not re-added by mistake.

6. Data Retention

Information is kept only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected.

Contact and Speaking Requests

Messages are kept for a reasonable period so that Gerald and his team can respond, follow up, and keep records of ongoing conversations.

Newsletter Subscribers

Subscriber email addresses are kept until the person unsubscribes or asks to be removed.

Server Logs

Basic technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and reliability purposes, then deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have specific rights over your personal information. These commonly include:

Right to Access

You can ask what personal information is held about you.

Right to Correct

You can ask to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to Delete

You can ask to delete personal information, subject to legal or operational limits.

Right to Object

You can object to certain uses of your information, such as marketing emails.

Right to Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

You can contact a privacy regulator in your jurisdiction if you believe your rights are being violated.

For California Residents

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA and CPRA, including the right to know, the right to delete, the right to correct, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. This website does not sell personal information.

For Visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

Where the GDPR applies, personal information is processed on the basis of consent, legitimate interest, or contract, as appropriate for the specific context.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has submitted personal information through this site, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and removed.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect the information that passes through this site. These measures include secure connections, limited access, and trusted service providers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While care is taken, no guarantee can be made that information will never be exposed through a breach or other event outside our control.

10. International Visitors

This website is operated from the United States. If you visit from another country, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

By using this site and submitting information, you understand that your information may be processed in the United States.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change as well. Material changes will be described clearly.

Continued use of the website after a change means that you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, or if you want to exercise a privacy right, please get in touch.

How to Reach Us

Use the contact page and include "Privacy Request" in your message so that it is routed correctly.
