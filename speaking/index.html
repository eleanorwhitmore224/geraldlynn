Speaking Overview

Clear Guidance for a Complicated System

Gerald brings years of frontline healthcare experience to every presentation. His talks are designed to help audiences understand the system, ask better questions, and take practical steps with confidence.

Whether your audience is made up of patients, caregivers, employers, librarians, healthcare professionals, or book club readers, Gerald tailors each event to the people in the room.

Signature Topics

What Gerald Speaks About

Every event is customized, but these are the themes audiences ask for most often.

Patient Advocacy

How patients and families can speak up, ask better questions, document care, and navigate confusing systems with confidence.

Healthcare Navigation

Practical tools for understanding coverage, preparing for appointments, organizing records, and reducing avoidable stress.

The Patient's Playbook

A plain-language walk through the core lessons of the book, with actionable takeaways audiences can use immediately.

Caregiver Support

Guidance for family members and caregivers who are managing appointments, medications, insurance questions, and care transitions.

Employer and HR Sessions

Help employees understand their benefits, use healthcare resources wisely, and communicate more effectively with providers.

Book Club and Library Events

Warm, discussion-friendly sessions for readers, librarians, and community groups exploring the book and its themes.

Formats

A Format That Fits Your Event

Gerald can join your event in person or remotely, depending on schedule and location. Each session is prepared with your audience in mind.

Keynote or Fireside Chat

A focused talk with time for Q and A. Best for conferences, employee events, community gatherings, and healthcare summits.

Workshop or Training Session

A practical, interactive session with worksheets, scenarios, and guided discussion. Best for smaller groups and teams.

Panel or Roundtable

Gerald joins other voices to discuss patient advocacy, healthcare access, communication, and system navigation.

Book Club or Library Visit

A relaxed conversation about the book, the writing process, and the questions readers care about most.

What to Include in Your Request

  • Event date and time
  • Event location or platform
  • Audience size and type
  • Preferred format
  • Topic or theme
  • Any deadlines or budget details
